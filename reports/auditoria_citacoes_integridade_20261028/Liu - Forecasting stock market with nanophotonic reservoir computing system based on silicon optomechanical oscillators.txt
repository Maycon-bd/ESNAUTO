Abstract: Theessenceofstockmarketforecastingistorevealtheintrinsicoperationrulesofstock
market, however it is a terribly arduous challenge for investors. The application of nanophotonic
technology in the intelligence field provides a new approach for stock market forecasting with
its unique advantages. In this work, a novel nanophotonic reservoir computing (RC) system
based on silicon optomechanical oscillators (OMO) with photonic crystal (PhC) cavities for
stock market forecasting is implemented. The long-term closing prices of four representative
stock indexes are accurately forecast with small prediction errors, and the forecasting results
with distinct characteristics are exhibited in the mature stock market and emerging stock market
separately. Our work offers solutions and suggestions for surmounting the concept drift problem
in stock market environment. The comprehensive influence of RC parameters on forecasting
performance are displayed via the mapping diagrams, while some intriguing results indicate that
the mature stock markets are more sensitive to the variation of RC parameters than the emerging
stock markets. Furthermore, the direction trend forecasting results illustrate that our system has
certain direction forecasting ability. Additionally, the stock forecasting problem with short listing
timeandfewdatainthestockmarketissolvedthroughtransferlearning(TL)instocksector. The
generalization ability (GA) of our nanophotonic reservoir computing system is also verified via
four stocks in the same region and industry. Therefore, our work contributes to a novel RC model
for stock market forecasting in the nanophotonic field, and provides a new prototype system for
more applications in the intelligent information processing field.

1. Introduction
Stockmarketisinextricablylinkedtothefinancialassetsofthestates,enterprisesandindividuals,
while its movement tendency is impacted by various macroeconomic and microeconomic factors
as diverse as political climate, interest rates, market news, fiscal policy, commodity price index,
investor psychology [1,2]. Accordingly in this sense, the prediction of stock market trend is
an extremely arduous challenge due to the stock market is generally noisy, complex, nonlinear,
nonparametric, and nonstationary in nature [3,4]. Conventional stock market prediction is
commonly based merely on economics, finance and simple mathematical statistical analysis.
Nevertheless, with the vigorous advance of artificial intelligence (AI) related technologies and
the soaring demand for stock market prediction, the stock market forecasting manipulated by
intelligent technologies has been evolved into a research upsurge in economic and financial fields
during the latest years.

The stock market prediction schemes based on intelligent technologies typically models the
historical data of stock price as time series, and deems the assumptions that historical tendency
of price repeats itself consistently and the stock price data contain all the fundamental factors
influencing the movements of stock market, which eludes the evaluation of empirical economic
factors [5,6]. According to such comprehension, stock market prediction systems with a variety
kindofintelligenttechnologieshasbeenproposedinsuccession,suchasartificialneuralnetworks
(ANN) [7,8], convolutional neural network (CNN) [9,10], recurrent neural network (RNN) [11],
support vector machine (SVM) [12,13], stochastic neural network (SNN) [14], and long-short
term memory (LSTM) networks [15]. However, there are more or less problems and defects in
exploiting a single intelligent technology to predict stock price data with different characteristics,
including local optimization, overfitting the learning model and parameter selection difficulties
[6,16]. Toremedythesedrawbacks,abatchoftechniciansadopthybridmechanismandensemble
method to synthesize two or more intelligent technologies for enhancing prediction capabilities
andgeneralizationperformance[6,17–20]. However, thesuccessoftheseintelligenttechnologies
in predicting the stock market commonly acquires to configure multilayer connection weights,
massive neurons and astonishing amount of hyper-parameters [21,22].
Instead of training vast weights in multiple layers, the reservoir computing (RC) as a particular
RNNmodelonlyrequirestotraintheoutputweightsexploitingpracticableoptimizationtechniques
while maintaining the weights of input layer and reservoir internal connection fixed randomly
[21,23]. Therefore, the RC can map input signals to high-dimensional space with simpler
learning algorithm, lower calculation power and faster learning process to complete prediction
and classification tasks in electronics, photonics, spintronics, mechanics, biology and other fields
[22–25]. Among them, the photonics-based implementation is one of the most perspective fields
for constructing high efficient RC owing to thoroughly utilize the advantages of optical intrinsic
properties, containing high data processing speed, large bandwidth, inherent parallelism, and low
energy consumption [24,26]. On this account, a mass of ingenious approaches for implementing
RC have been gradually proposed based on both spatially distributed and time delayed photonic
nodes [24,25,27].
The RC composed of spatially distributed nonlinear photonic nodes allows for a variety of
connection topologies using integrated photonic devices on chip, standard diffractive optical
elements (DOE), and excitable photonic devices, along with reflecting its parallel processing
capability by dealing with large-scale input data without immolating calculation velocity
[24,26–32]. Another RC is constituted by a single nonlinear node with one or more time-delayed
optoelectronicorall-opticalfeedbackforobtainingtime-multiplexedvirtualnodesinthetemporal
domain, in which the nonlinearity alternatively originates from either the discrete element in the
feedback loop or the laser response as the feedback delay signal reenters its cavity [24,25]. A
number of practical implementations based on vertical cavity surface emitting lasers (VCSEL),
semiconductoropticalamplifier(SOA),semiconductorringlaser(SRL),semiconductornanolaser
(SNL), and Fabry-Perot laser (FPL) etc. fall into this time-delayed RC [33–37]. Compared
to these conventional semiconductor laser (SL), the silicon optomechanical oscillators (OMO)
characterized by high quality factor and small mode volume have some unique advantages of
small device size, low cost, low power consumption, high scalability, good complementary metal
oxide semiconductor (CMOS) fabrication compatibility, favourable monolithic integration and
massproduction[38–42]. Furthermore,diversenonlineardynamicsofsiliconOMOderivedfrom
the strong optomechanical coupling and optical confinement have been successfully observed in
recent works [43–46]. In these cases, the above advantages and nonlinearity of the silicon OMO
make these oscillators good potential candidates for implementing RC. However, to the best of
our knowledge, we have noted that the investigations about nanophotonic reservoir computing
based on silicon OMO remained scarce, and especially there are no reports on its application in
forecasting stock market.

In this paper, a novel nanophotonic reservoir computing system based on silicon OMO with
photonic crystal (PhC) cavities for stock market forecasting is implemented. Firstly, we introduce
the time-delayed feedback mechanism and the injection signal affected by the stock market
information into the silicon-based OMO to construct the nanophotonic reservoir computing
systemwithcompetenceinpredictingstockmarket. Inordertoensuretheaccuracyofprediction,
theoriginalstockmarketdataisnormalized, andthenthenormalizeddataisdenoisedbydiscrete
wavelet transform (DWT). Four globally representative stock indices are selected, and their data
are collected for the training and testing process of the forecasting task. This paper presents the
long-term prediction results of these four stock indices, and reflects the prediction performance
through four relevant error evaluation metrics. On the basis of above, we study the impact
of concept drift on the prediction performance of four stock indices over time, and depict the
corresponding conclusions. Moreover, the influence of injected light wavelength and injected
light power of silicon OMO on the prediction performance is demonstrated by the mapping
diagrams, while the effect of time-delayed feedback intensity and injection signal intensity of
siliconOMOonthepredictionperformanceisalsostudiedviathemappingdiagrams. According
to the latest data updated every day, we continuously track and forecast the four stock indices in
theonerecentyear,andcomparethedailyforecastresultswiththerealvaluesofthestockmarket,
together with statistically analyzing the consistency of their rising and falling trends. Moreover,
we explore the capacity of transfer learning (TL) in nanophotonic reservoir computing system
based on silicon OMO, i.e., embodied by using the massive data accumulated for a long time in
the stock sector to predict the stock prices of several companies with short listing time under this
sector. This has important strategic significance and practical value for predicting stocks with
short issuance time and less time series data. Ultimately, we discuss the generalization ability
(GA) of nanophotonic reservoir computing system based on silicon OMO, which is reflected by
training any one of the four selected stocks in the same region and industry to predict the other
three stocks.
The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, the system scheme is
introduced in detail. In Section 3, the selection and setting of stock market data associating
with the measurement indicators of its prediction performance are illustrated. In Section 4, the
forecastingresultsandtheinfluenceofsystemparametersarepresented. InSection5,thetransfer
learningandgeneralizationabilityofstockpredictionsystemareexploredanddiscussed. Section
6 summarizes the results and provides advices for further research.
2. System scheme
The schematic diagram of nanophotonic reservoir computing system based on silicon OMO with
PhC cavities for forecasting stock market is elaborated in Fig. 1. The whole system is comprised
of three main parts: the input layer, reservoir, and output layer. In the input layer, the original
stockmarketdataiscollectedandrecordedas u(n)withaperiodoftimeduration T,where nisthe
discrete time index. Henceforth theu(n)is transformed into preprocessed stock market dataU(n)
through pretreatment operations of normalization and wavelet denoising. A chaotic mask signal
M(t)with identical time durationT generated by our previous work [47] is exploited to guarantee
the variability of input signal over the distinct virtual nodes where the information is read out
[33,48]. On this foundation, theU(n)is multiplied byM(t)and rescaled within appropriate range
to acquire the masked input signalS(t), thereafter theS(t) is transmitted into the reservoir via
modulation. In essence, the stock price information of input signal is hidden in the amplitude
of masked signal per period after the input layer processed. In the reservoir, the injection laser
(I-Laser) is utilized as the input light source to stimulate the silicon OMO. And the physical
fabrication parameters corresponding to the silicon OMO have been elaborated in literatures [43]
and [39]. The reservoir is a master-slave configuration constructed by silicon OMO injected by a
drive laser (D-Laser), where the output signal of I-Laser and the modulated signal of D-Laser

[PÁGINA PDF 4]
Research Article Vol. 30, No. 13 / 20 Jun 2022 / Optics Express 23362
enter the silicon OMO as one signal through the beam combiner (BC). The states with a time
interval θ output by silicon OMO constitutes the virtual network nodes. There areL virtual
nodes at each intervalθ in the feedback loop with a delay timeτ. This feedback delay timeτ
nearly matches the input time durationT (= Lθ) in the case of de-synchronization scheme, i.e.,
τ = T + θ [23,33,34]. In the output layer, all the states of virtual nodesx(n) corresponding to
the transient response of silicon OMO are read out for training the output weightWout, and the
ultimate outputy(n) are weighted and linearly summed up, i.e.,y(n) = ∑︁x(n)Wout. Notably, the
optimal output weights are calculated by exploiting linear least-squares method in this paper for
minimizing the mean-square error between the anticipant target values and RC output values
during the training stage.

3. Stock market data and evaluation metrics
3.1. Stock market data selection and setting
In the stock market, the overall level of various stock prices and their variations are generally
reflected by the stock indices, which can provide the dynamic information and development
form of the stock market. The maturity of stock market is determined by multiple factors such
as operation mechanism, market scale, competition degree and order degree, thus different
market maturities may affect the accuracy of the prediction model. After several hundred years
of successive development, European and American stock markets have become mature stock
marketswithsophisticatedmarketoperationsystem,hugemarketscaleandreasonablesupervision
institution. In contrast, the stock market in mainland China has a relatively short development
time while the market operation and supervision system are imperfect. Although it is developing
rapidly, the stock market in mainland China is still in the developing stage and is an emerging
stock market. In this work, we select two of the most representative stock indices in mainland
China to delegate the emerging stock market, namely Shanghai Stock Exchange Composite
Index (SHSECI, stock symbol (SS): 000001) and Shenzhen Stock Exchange Component Index
(SZSECI, SS: 399001). Besides, we also select the National Association of Securities Dealers
Automated Quotations (NASDAQ) Composite Index (NASDAQCI, SS: IXIC), one of the most
representative indexes in Americas, and the London Financial Times Stock Exchange 100 Index
(LFTSE100I, SS: FTSE), one of the most representative indexes in Europe, as the delegate stock
indexes of mature stock market. The historical daily closing prices of these four representative
stock indexes are collected from the public financial data services of Royalflush client software
providedbyHithinkRoyalflushInformationNetworkCo.,Ltd. Detailsofthefourselectedindexes

4.2. Concept drift and stock market forecast
Concept drift phenomenon constantly appears in stock market technical analysis owing to the
financial time series is a special dynamic data stream, whose potential statistical characteristics
change in an unpredictable way over time, resulting in the continuous depreciation of the
knowledge learned from historical data analysis, thus reducing the prediction accuracy of the
constructed model. Evidently, it is a vital issue to detect and surmount the concept drift in stock
analysis in time. However, there are rare studies on maintaining the prediction accuracy of
learned model in the operation process.

5. Transfer learning and generalization ability
5.1. Transfer learning in stock sector
Stocks with short issuance time and few available data are often encountered in stock market
forecasting. It is difficult for the forecasting system to predict these stocks with little data
effectively by utilizing the previous training and testing methods. Nevertheless, transfer learning,
whichappliesthelearnedknowledgeinthesourcedomaintothenewtargetdomain,canskillfully
solve the above difficult problem, since the stock price changes of companies in the same stock
sector and the stock sector itself often have correlation and linkage.
In this work, the capacity of transfer learning capacity in nanophotonic reservoir computing
system based on silicon OMO is explored by exploiting the massive data accumulated for a long
time in the agricultural service stock sector (ASSS, SS: 881104) to forecast the stock prices

6. Conclusion
In this work, we implement a novel nanophotonic reservoir computing system based on silicon
OMO using PhC cavities for stock market forecasting. The nanophotonic reservoir computing
system based on silicon OMO with time-delayed feedback mechanism and DWT denoising is
successfully established for the first time. The long-term closing prices of SHSECI, SZSECI,
NASDAQCI, and LFTSE100I are precisely forecast with tiny NMSE, RMSE, MAE, and MAPE,
revealing the stock forecast of mature stock market and emerging stock market has disparate
characteristics. Meanwhile,thecomparisonresultsshowthatoursystemcanachievehigh-quality
stock index prediction as the listed literature. The influence of concept drift phenomenon on
forecasting performance in stock market is investigated by changing training days, and we find
that the good prediction performance prefers to appear in the range of 2000 to 4000 training
days for the fixed 1000 test days. Moreover, the overall impact of RC parameters on forecasting
performanceareexhibitedthroughthemappingdiagramsofforecasterrorsintheparameterspace
(ξw,Pin)and( kinj,kfb), wheretheinterestingvariationprocessesareresembletothecoastlineand
archipelago in the geographic map. The results of these two mapping diagrams also illustrate
the different variation regulations for mature stock market and emerging stock market, while the
mature stock markets are more sensitive to the change of RC parameters than the emerging stock
markets. The direction trend forecasting results show that our direction forecasting performance
is similar to the prediction system based on single intelligence technology in the literature, and
miserably worse than the hybrid prediction system. In addition, the four stocks RPBT, DYWS,
JHBT, and KQBL with short issuance time and few data from ASSS are effectively forecast via
transfer learning, which indicate the transfer learning capacity of our nanophotonic reservoir
computing system. The other four stocks EMI, HTS, SHCF, and EBS in the securities industry
of Shanghai are availably forecast with each other, revealing the generalization ability of our
nanophotonic reservoir computing system.
Although the above summary illustrates the achievements of this work, there are still some
limitations in this work that need to be investigated in the future. In the first instance, explore
optical means that can improve the feature extraction and analysis ability of the nanophotonic
reservoir computing system, or cooperate with other optical computing systems to construct
hybrid forecasting systems, such as the optical neural networks (ONNs) based on programmable
nanophotonic processor with deep learning application potential [49] and the integrated photonic
tensor core based on optical soliton frequency comb with parallel convolutional processing

capacity [50]. The second aspect is that the present dataset only covers the closing prices of
four stock indices, one stock sector, and eight individual stocks. It is interesting to study more
regionally representative stock indexes around the world with its open, high, and low prices
etc. Meantime, the more individual stocks in diverse stock sectors, industries, and regions are
waiting to explore more capabilities of the RC system and discover more meaningful financial
phenomena. Besides, the research object can not only be limited to the stock market, but also
focus on the futures market, option market, bond market, fund market, foreign exchange market
and etc. The third facet is that with the improvement of feature extraction and analysis ability,
more relevant information sources can be utilized to enhance the prediction ability of the stock
market, such as financial news, macroeconomic factors, corporate disclosure news, investors
sentiments, and even Internet social platform information. The final aspect is to improve the
forecasting accuracy of stock market direction trend and construct practical trading strategies for
portfolio management, which is the ultimate goal and challenge for all the investors. Desirably
from these research advices to inspire scholars for splendid and wide-vision works in the future.
