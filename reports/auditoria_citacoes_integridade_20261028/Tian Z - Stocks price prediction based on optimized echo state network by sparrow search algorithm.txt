Stocks price prediction based on optimized echo state network
by sparrow search algorithm
Zhengjie Tian 1 · Lijun Pei 1
Received: 19 November 2023 / Revised: 7 February 2024 / Accepted: 8 February 2024 / Published online: 11 March 2024

Abstract
The stock market reﬂects the level of economic development of a country to a certain extent. Therefore, stock price forecasting
has become a popular research issue. More and more attention has been paid to the establishment of stock price forecasting
models. However, the stock market is a nonlinear system ﬁlled with high noise, and the traditional fundamental analysis,
technical analysis and ARIMA model cannot achieve the ideal prediction result. Although traditional neural networks have
good adaptability and nonlinear approximation capability, but they also have drawbacks, such as slow training speed and
easiness to fall into local optimization. The echo state network–sparrow search algorithm (SSA–ESN) model employed in
this paper uses the sparrow search algorithm to optimize the parameters of echo state network (ESN). It has not only better
prediction result but also inheriting the excellent properties of ESN. The SSA–ESN model is used to predict several stocks
price, and the results are compared with those of BP neural network, Elman neural network, LSTM and echo state network.
And the prediction accuracy of SSA–ESN model is signiﬁcantly better than those of the other four prediction models.
Keywords Stocks price · Sparrow search algorithm · Echo state network
1 Introduction
The stocks market serve as a barometer of a country’s
economic development, to some extent. In recent years,
numerous scholars have conducted diverse studies on the
stocks market, proposing a range of analytical and fore-
casting methods to address its unique characteristics. These
methods include fundamental analysis, technical analysis
and time series analysis. However, the stocks market is a
dynamic, nonlinear system with high noise, which poses
challenges for the application of traditional prediction meth-
ods.
Neural networks have gained popularity in stocks price
prediction due to their exceptional adaptability and non-
linear approximation capabilities. While traditional neural
networks like BP neural networks exhibit impressive perfor-
mance, they also display signiﬁcant limitations such as slow
training speeds, tendency toward local optimization and vul-
nerability to noise.

Computational intelligence is a collective term for algo-
rithms developed by scholars through computer implementa-
tions of biological intelligence. This ﬁeld provides numerous
optimization methods for time series prediction and has
numerous successful applications in dealing with massive
data simulations. However, individual intelligent algorithms
often fall short in their applicability to a wide range of
problems, exhibiting limitations in both theory and practice.
Therefore, combining intelligent optimization algorithms
with neural networks has become the mainstream approach
for stocks price prediction among scholars today. This paper
adheres to such an idea, based on a special neural network–e-
cho state network, using the sparrow search algorithm to
optimize its learning rate and regularization coefﬁcient, to
improve the prediction accuracy of the echo state network
on the basis of trying to maintain its original operational
efﬁciency. SSA–ESN shows a greater advantage in predic-
tion accuracy compared to BP neural networks, Elman neural
networks, LSTM and echo state networks.
In recent years, different methods have been proposed for
stocks price forecasting. The ARIMA and GARCH mod-
els had been widely used for ﬁnancial market forecasting
[1]. However, due to the complexity and nonlinearity of the
time series of stocks price, many traditional models cannot

two hybrid models for stocks price prediction: the ARIMA-
MLP model and the MLP-ARIMA model [ 8]. Bose et al.
emphasized the importance of dimensionality reduction and

of stocks [ 9]. Wang et al. proposed an interval variational
mode decomposition (FIVMD) method with feedback mech-
anism to model interval trends and residuals, respectively,
through optimization algorithms and deep learning models to
improve prediction accuracy [10]. Du used arbitrage pricing
model to establish the relationship between various system
factors and adjusted stock prices [11]. Kumar et al. proposed
a hybrid long short-term memory network model based on
adaptive particle swarm optimization to predict three major
stock indexes in the short and long term [ 12]. Teng et al.
proposed a multi-scale local cue and attention-based LSTM
model (MLCA–LSTM) to capture potential price trend pat-
terns [ 13]. Wang et al. introduced time series technology
to conduct comprehensive modeling of stock price data and
established TSC model to detect the uncertainty of data
changes [14]. Liu et al. proposed a new stock price prediction
model VML, which uses MAML algorithm and long short-
term memory (LSTM) network to predict subsequences [15].
The literature review indicates that it has become a
mainstream to combine multiple models or to employ com-
putational intelligence to optimize the hyperparameters of
neural networks to improve the accuracy of stocks price pre-
diction. However, the most convenient network is prioritized
when all networks are equally feasible. Echo state network is
a new recursive neural network algorithm proposed by Jaeger
in 2001. Jaeger used the ESN to forecast the time series and
got better results. Jaeger compared it with forward type neu-
ral network and traditional recursive neural network. In the
meanwhile, Jaeger gave the algorithmic rules and training
mode of ESN [ 16]. It is a special kind of neural network
with good short-term memory, and its simple training pro-
cess and unique way of constructing the reservoir pool make
its training efﬁciency greatly improved, which is widely used
in time series prediction. ESN has a good function approx-
imation capability, which can effectively solve the serious
nonlinearities of the stocks market. ESN has a great advan-
tage over traditional neural networks in terms of stability,
global optimality, local minimum problem and the complex-
ity of the training process. Compared with traditional neural
networks, ESN has great improvement.
Skowronski and Harri applied ESNs to automatic lan-
guage recognition classiﬁcation [ 17]. Lin argued that the
random selection of the initial values of network weights
made the network prediction results unstable and used the
Hurst index to set the weights of ESN [ 18]. However, Ishu
et al. argued that ESN does not need to rely on a large
amount of experience as traditional neural networks do [ 19].
Zhang proposed a generalized model for short-term stocks
price prediction based on echo state network [ 20]. Zhang
et al. used a synthetic model of phase space reconstruction,
ESN, and moving average convergence/divergence (MACD)
for stocks prediction and compared it with other models,
such as backpropagation neural network (BPNN), support
vector machine (SVM), hidden Markov model (HMM) and
programmed neural network (PNN), to validate the high efﬁ-
ciency of the composite model consisting of ESN for stocks
price prediction [ 21].
Sparrow search algorithm (SSA) is a novel intelligent
optimization algorithm proposed by Xue et al. based on
the predatory behavior of sparrow population [ 22]. Com-
pared with traditional intelligent algorithms, it has a strong
global search capability. Liu et al. introduced the sparrow
search algorithm to optimize the hyperparameters of LSTM
model to improve the accuracy of stocks trend prediction
[23]. Sparrow search algorithm to optimize neural network
for regression prediction is also applied in other ﬁelds. Liu
et al. combined SVM model and SSA to predict the caloriﬁc
value and nitrogen content based on feedstock properties and
pyrolysis conditions [ 24]. Liao proposed a load forecasting
model incorporating improved sparrow search algorithm and
long and short-term memory neural network (LSTM) [ 25].
Tan et al. optimized PSR and ESN by combining TVFEMD,
IASSA and achieved more accurate wind power prediction
[26]. Zheng et al. proposed BP neural network model opti-
mized based on SSA. Compared with the traditional BP
neural network, it has higher accuracy and better ﬁtting result
in ship trajectory prediction [ 27].
To summarize, although traditional neural networks such
as BP neural networks reﬂect advantages in predicting stocks

layer, a reservoir pool and an output layer.

network. But its size is much larger than that of a traditional
neural network hidden layer, usually around tens or hundreds.
Its internal neurons are sparsely connected, and the sparsity
SD is generally taken as 2%-5%.

2.2 Sparrow search algorithm (SSA)
SSA algorithm is a swarm intelligence optimization algo-
rithm based on sparrow foraging and predator avoidance
behaviors. Sparrows are divided into discoverers and fol-
lowers when foraging. The discoverers are responsible for

ﬁnding food in the group, providing foraging areas and
directions to the whole sparrow group. And the followers fol-
low the discoverers to obtain food. In order to obtain food,
sparrows can generally adopt two behavioral strategies for
foraging: discoverer and follower. Individuals within the pop-
ulation will monitor the behavior of other individuals within
the population, and aggressors within the population will
increase their predation rate by competing for food resources
with their high-intake peers. In addition, sparrow populations
will engage in antipredatory behavior when they recognize
danger.
In SSA, each individual (sparrow) has a position that rep-
resents its location in the search space. In each iteration,
the position of each individual is updated based on the ﬁt-
ness value. Because the discoverer is responsible for ﬁnding
food for the entire sparrow population and providing forag-
ing directions to all followers, the discoverer can obtain a
larger foraging search range than the followers.

2.3 SSA–ESN model construction and algorithm
steps
Since the regularization coefﬁcients and learning rates of
ESN are generally set empirically or randomly, the network
structure has a certain degree of randomness. In this paper, the
SSA algorithm is applied to the optimization of the regular-
ization coefﬁcient and learning rate to improve the prediction
accuracy of the ESN model. Figure 2 demonstrates the net-
work structure of SSA–ESN.
The steps of the SSA–ESN algorithm are as follows,
Step 1 input samples, normalize the data to the [0,1] inter-
val;
Step 2 divide the training set and test set according to the
ratio;
Step 3 choose the reservoir pool size, reservoir pool spar-
sity. Randomly generate W
in and W , and use ESN to train
Wout. Set the value range of regularization factor and learning
rate;
Step 4 optimize the regularization factor and learning rate
using SSA to ﬁnd their optimal values;
Step 5 return the optimal regularization coefﬁcients and
learning rate to the echo State Network and utilize it for
prediction.

3 Model assumptions, data preprocessing
and experimental conﬁguration
3.1 Model assumptions
If the future has nothing to do with the past, and the patterns
of the past will not repeat themselves in the future, then fore-
casting is all but impossible, and stock price forecasting is
out of the question.
The model used in this paper is based on the following
assumptions.
1. Stocks price show certain patterns, and past patterns will
repeat themselves in the present and future.
2. Stocks price reﬂects the combined effects of national
policies, stockholders’ psychology, and corporate deci-
sions on the stock market.
3.2 Data preprocessing
The data we obtain are sound, real and anomalous, so the data
are not cleaned in this paper. However, in order to unify the
basic measurement unit of data, speed up the convergence
of the network and improve the generalization ability of the
model, we carry out the normalization of the data. In fact,
normalization of data is a common way of processing data.
It maps the input data to a suitable range, avoids the gradient
too large or too small, and helps to improve the stability of
training.

6 Conclusion
The prediction of stocks prices is a major concern for a
wide range of ﬁnancial practitioners. The use of models with
higher prediction accuracy facilitates more accurate ﬁnan-
cial decisions. A large amount of the literatures indicate that
neural networks have higher prediction accuracy compared
to traditional stocks price prediction methods. However, tra-
ditional neural network models have two major drawbacks
for accurately predicting stocks price: one is that traditional
neural networks are very sensitive to noise, and the other
is that the initial parameters of traditional neural networks
are often set empirically, and the randomness tends to have
a great impact on the prediction accuracy. To address the
ﬁrst drawback of traditional neural networks, in this paper
we use ESN to adapt to the high noise and severe nonlinear-
ity of the stocks price market. For the second disadvantage
of traditional neural networks, in this paper we use compu-
tational intelligence, combine the sparrow search algorithm
with ESN, and optimizes its initial parameters, in order to
avoid the decrease in prediction accuracy of traditional neu-
ral networks due to the randomness of the initial parameters.
In this paper, we investigate the prediction ability of the
combined sparrow search algorithm and echo state network
model in the stock market. BP neural network, Elman neu-
ral network, LSTM, ESN and SSA–ESN are compared. The
prediction results of 20 active stocks in China and the USA
prove that ESN has more accurate prediction results in stocks
price prediction, and the use of the sparrow search algorithm
can effectively improve the prediction performance of ESN.
The innovation of this paper is that the sparrow search
algorithm and echo state network are successfully combined
to deal with the stock price prediction with nonlinearity and
high noise, and the prediction accuracy of echo state network
is improved. At the same time, SSA–ESN model also inherits
the advantages of echo state network, such as strong general-
ization ability, good stability, short running time, simple and
effective model.
Financial practitioners can formulate investment strate-
gies according to the forecast results of SSA–ESN, and can
also use SSA–ESN model to evaluate the optimal strategy
of the given investment strategy. In addition, the SSA–ESN
model can also be combined with existing ﬁnancial analysis
tools. Here, are a few ways of combination:
1. Feature engineering: consider the feature variables used
by existing ﬁnancial analysis tools and combine them

example, existing ﬁnancial analysis tools may use techni-
cal indicators (such as moving averages, relative strength
indicators, etc.) as feature variables, which can be used
as additional feature variables in the SSA–ESN.
2. Model integration: the SSA–ESN can be integrated with
other predictive models in existing ﬁnancial analysis
tools. The prediction results of different models can
be combined through voting, weighted average, etc., to
improve the overall prediction accuracy.
3. Risk management: use the risk management module pro-
vided by existing ﬁnancial analysis tools to measure and
manage the risk of forecast outcomes. By analyzing the
error, conﬁdence interval and other risk indicators, the
prediction results can be better evaluated and the corre-
sponding risk management strategies can be adopted.
However, there are some limitations to this study. It
will be further discussion to optimize more parameters
and explore its internal mechanism. The combination of
advanced algorithms and data processing methods, such as
modal decomposition, will also become a major trend in the
ﬁeld of ﬁnancial forecasting.
