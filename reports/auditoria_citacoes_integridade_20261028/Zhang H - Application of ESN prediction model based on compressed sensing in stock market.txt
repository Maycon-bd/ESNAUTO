Echo State Network (ESN) is often used for time series prediction and chaotic series pre- 
diction . But in the training process of ESN model, the reserve pool will involve a large 
number of calculations, so the reserve pool may have node redundancy, resulting in inac- 
curate training model. It is very important to select the active nodes in the reserve pool 
because the predicted nodes are very few compared with all the nodes in the reserve pool. 
Based on the characteristics of the original vector, compressed sensing can reduce the di- 
mension of the vector so as to obtain the activated nodes. In this paper, the ESN model is 
added with compressed sensing, which simpliﬁes the reserve pool part of the ESN model. 
The simulation results show that our method can not only reduce the calculation amount 
of training effectively, but also improve the accuracy.

1. Introduction 
ESN is a new recursive neural network proposed by Jaeger in 2001 [1] , which can avoid the slow convergence speed, high 
computational complexity and instability of the recursive neural network learning algorithm [2,3] . So ESN becomes a hot 
issue in academic community, and is widely used in various ﬁelds, including dynamic pattern classiﬁcation, robot control, 
object tracking and moving target detection, event monitoring, etc. [4,5] , especially on the time series prediction problem, 
made outstanding contributions. And the ESN in time series prediction and chaotic prediction have done a lot of work, but 
because of the reserve pool of ESN model involves a large amount of calculation, the ESN training and prediction for a long 
time [6] . ESN also has many algorithms, such as Pseudo inverse method, Wiener-Hopf method, Ridge Regression (Tikhonov 
regularization), etc [7] . 
The compressed sensing (CS) proposed by Tao can obtain the discrete samples of the signal under the condition that 
the sampling rate is much lower than Nyquist sampling rate, so as to ensure the signal reconstruction without distortion 
[8] . The key idea of compressed sensing theory mainly includes two points. The ﬁrst is the sparse structure of the signal. 
The traditional Shannon signal representation method only uses the least priori information of sampled signals, namely 
the bandwidth of signals. However, many of the signals that attract much attention in real life have some structural char- 
acteristics of their own [9–11] . Compared with the DOF(Degree of Freedom) of bandwidth information, these structural

sample data. Because compression has stability (acquisition and reconstruction processes are both numerically stable); gen- 
erality (the same measurement projection can be used for different signal acquisition); asymmetry (encoding (sampling) is 
simple, most of the calculation is decoding (reconstruction)); democracy (each measurement carries the same information, 
which is robust to the loss at the time of measurement). Therefore, the trained ESN reserve pool is reduced in dimension 
by using compressed sensing to retain only the nodes with the highest value (feature nodes), that is, the activated nodes 
are screened out and the activated nodes are used for prediction [12,13] . Since only active nodes are used in the prediction 
process, as long as the model is trained well, the prediction speed will be greatly accelerated. Moreover, the interference 
of other inactive nodes is relatively small, which can make the error of the whole model smaller, and the mean value and 
variance value of the error will also be reduced [14] . 
In this paper, an ESN prediction model based on compressed sensing is proposed to get better prediction results with 
fewer nodes. The main contributions of this paper are as follows: 
1. A new algorithm is proposed to optimize the ESN reserve pool using compressed sensing to improve the prediction 
speed and accuracy. 
2. Analyze the predicted data and compare the advantages and disadvantages of the original ESN model and the ESN model 
with CS added. 
3. Including the use of conventional chaotic systems for simulation veriﬁcation, we also choose more complex stock data 
to verify the effectiveness and accuracy of the proposed model. The simulation results are satisfactory. 
The rest of this article is as follows. Section 2 describes ESN model and compressed sensing model. Section 3 will intro- 
duce the new ESN model with compression sensing, and give its ﬂow chart and algorithm. Section 4 will give two simula- 
tions, one is the prediction of Logistic chaotic time series, the other is the prediction of 200 stocks. Finally, we provide a 
summary of the whole work. 
2. Preliminary 
2.1. Echo state network 
2.1.1. The structure of the ESN 
ESN consists of three parts: 1) an input layer; 2) a reserve pool; 3) an output layer. Where, W in connects the input layer 
and the reserve layer, W reserve layer internal connection, W out connects the reserve layer and the output layer. Fig. 1 shows 
the basic structure of ESN. W in , W and the reserve pool are initialized without training. We only need to train the connection 
between the reserve layer and the output layer W out . Since nodes generated by dynamic reservoirs have short-term memory 
capacity, we can map data from the lower dimension to the higher dimension, and then reduce the higher dimension data 
to the speciﬁed dimension by W out , so as to realize the prediction process. 
Since the ESN prediction model needs to raise the dimension through the reserve pool and reducing the dimension, we 
need to activate the function at both the reserve pool and the output layer, and the updated rules are as follows:

2.2. Compressed sensing 
2.2.1. The basic idea of compressed sensing 
According to the Nyquist sampling theorem, in order for the sampled digital signal to retain all the information in the 
original signal, the sampling frequency must be more than 2 times of the highest frequency in the signal. Compressed sens- 
ing believes that if the signal is sparse, it can be reconstructed and restored by sampling points far below the requirements 
of the sampling theorem. The key to the breakthrough is the sampling mode, and when we say “sampling frequency”, that 
means sampling at equal intervals, which is what digital signals usually do, and that also obeys the Nyquist sampling theo- 
rem. If random subsampling is adopted, then the frequency domain will no longer be extended with a ﬁxed period, but will 
produce a large number of irrelevant disturbance values. The largest peaks are still faintly visible, and these interference 
values are somewhat overwritten, which look like random noise and are actually caused by the non-zero energy leakage of 
several original signals. This can be understood as the random sampling makes the spectrum no longer move in a neat way, 
but move randomly in a small part. The frequency leakage is uniformly distributed in the whole frequency domain, so the 
leakage value is relatively small, which makes the recovery possible. 
The preconditions of compressed sensing are as follows: 1) The signal is sparse in the frequency domain. The signal 
needs to be sparse in a transform domain, but usually the signal will not be completely sparse . In fact, as long as it 
approximately meets the sparsity, in other words, most values tend to zero and only a small number of large non-zero 
values, it can be considered as a compressible signal, and CS subsampling can be carried out on it. If it is not sparse in the 
frequency domain, we can do discrete wavelet transform (DWT), discrete cosine transform (DCT) and so on to ﬁnd its sparse 
transform. 2) Random subsampling. It has been pointed out above that in the case of random subsampling, the frequency

2.2.2. Reconstruction of compressed sensing 
If a signal is sparse in a transformation domain, then an observation matrix unrelated to the transformation basis can be 
used to project the higher dimension obtained from the transformation to a lower dimension space, and then the original 
signal can be reconstructed from the small number of projected values with high probability by solving an optimization 
problem [17,18] . Reconstruction of CS is the method to solve underdetermined equations y = /Theta1· s . This is a zero norm (L 0 ) 
minimization problem, and an NP complete problem (no quick solution problem), therefore tends to be converted to a norm 
(L 1 ) minimization solution, or some approximate estimation algorithms are used [8,19,20] . 
Orthogonal Matching Pursuit algorithm is one of the commonly used algorithms in CS reconstruction algorithm. Its main 
ideas are as follows:

3. ESN model with compressed sensing 
The nodes of the reserve pool in ESN model are very many, so when it comes to the operation of the reserve pool, 
both the operation speed and the accuracy of the operation will deﬁnitely be seriously affected. Because the reserve pool 
node number directly affects how well the ESN model to predict nonlinear data, we can’t reduce the reserve pool of nodes 
directly. Compressed sensing can extract node features, so you can use compressed sensing to reduce the number of nodes 
in the reserve pool. Due to a small number of nodes contain all the features of the original node, which can be used to 
describe the original node. In fact, this process is the recovery process of compressed sensing. The nodes extracted from 
the ESN model can not only ensure the accuracy of the original model, but also enhance its robustness and speed up of its 
prediction. 
Only the connection matrix ( W out ) from the reserve pool to the output layer needs training in the ESN model. If the 
nodes after feature extraction are used to train ESN model, which will improve the weight distribution of feature nodes 
during the training process. This makes the model training process more stable. The ESN model with compressed sensing 
(for short: CS-ESN model) can be represented as Fig. 4 . A compression layer was added to the original ESN model, where 
the inverse process of compressed sensing is used. The characteristics of the original reserve pool node x were extracted 
to obtain cx, and then cx was used to train the output layer connection matrix W out . The W out trained by this method has 
stronger stability and faster prediction speed. 
We assume that the reserve pool dimension of the original ESN model is N, and the reserve pool dimension after dimen- 
sion reduction through compressed sensing is CN. In other words, we ﬁrst get the original reserve pool data x according to 
compressed sensing and the sparse basis /Psi1, the measurement matrix /Phi1. Then, cx is obtained by compression of x using 
compressed sensing. That’s the Eq. (10) . 
cx = /Phi1/Psi1· x (10) 
By compression sensing, W in ∈ P CN×K , W ∈ P C N×C N , W out ∈ P L ×CN . The iterative formula is Eq. (11) . 
x (n +1) = f(W in · u (n +1) + W · x (n ) )

5. Conclusion 
In this paper, a new ESN model is proposed, and compressed sensing is added on the original ESN prediction model. 
The purpose of extracting the characteristics of the reserve pool node is to reduce the reserve pool node. Since the feature 
nodes of the reserve pool are extracted, most of the features of the original reserve pool are retained. Therefore, the CS-ESN 
model can ensure the accuracy of prediction and even have a better effect than the original ESN model. It can be seen 
from the simulation that both the prediction accuracy and the prediction stability have been improved greatly. Through the 
simulation of chaotic series prediction and stock prediction, both the average value of prediction error and the variance of
